# Afterhours 16 — v0.4.5 Patch Notes

v0.4.5 is the biggest update yet. It adds a new park event, crews with their own clubhouse and a full career mode, 32 new animation packages, and an overhaul of badges, stamina and defense. It closes with a performance and polish pass.

---

## New modes and places

### The Pro Run (career mode)
- **A career in the Afterhours Pro League:** 32 original teams, two conferences and full 15-man rosters.
- **College and the draft:**
  - Pick one of 12 colleges and play 3 games. How you play sets your draft stock.
  - Draft Night runs two rounds of 32, and the team holding your slot takes you.
- **The season:**
  - An 82-game season, a play-in, four playoff rounds and the Finals.
  - Awards: MVP, Finals MVP, Rookie of the Year, Defensive Player of the Year and the scoring title.
- **Play or sim** any of your games. Sim settings cover quarter length, difficulty, your minutes, series length, the play-in, upsets and home court.
- **Rewards:** games you play pay **VC and badge progress at 1.5x** the park rate.
- **No music** in Pro Run games, like Pro-Am.

### The King Tut Cup (park event)
- **The park:** a glow-in-the-dark park among the pyramids, with neon court lines, sphinxes, obelisks, a mini-golf strip and laser-tag barriers.
- **Every game is an ante-up** (500 to 10,000 VC):
  - Win, and you take the other side's stake too, boosted by your Cup win streak.
  - Lose or leave, and the stake is gone.
- **A 48-hour leaderboard** of net VC won, against about 200 AI hoopers. A neon billboard on the plaza shows the top five and you.
- **Prizes:** the glow-in-the-dark **mo-cap suit**, Pharaoh shoes, the Pharaoh celebration, the Scarab headband and up to 150,000 VC.
- **New in the final touches:** a gold **sphinx** at center court on every Cup court.

### Crews and the Crew HQ
- **Crews:** start one with up to 39 of your friends, with a name, tag and color.
- **40 crew levels** (level rewards are coming soon).
- **Crew XP:**
  - Every game earns crew XP: about 30% of the Rep it pays, weighted by mode.
  - It's doubled when a crew member runs with you.
  - Your members earn crew XP while you're away, too.
- **The Crew HQ** is on every park's plaza:
  - a full court and a shootaround court in your crew's colors;
  - your online members hanging out and getting shots up;
  - members and level boards;
  - **5-on-5 crew runs** once four members are on.

### Streak visuals (every park)
- After 3 straight wins, a **wall of fire** rises around the court. It grows at 6, 9 and 12 wins.
- In the King Tut Cup, the same steps bring green lasers instead.

## Gameplay

### Shooting
- **Green windows are 10% smaller** across the board: jumpers, free throws and layups.
- **Timed layups.** Hold the shot button through the gather and let go at the top; the shot meter shows the window.
  - Each layup package has its own timing: Scoop comes out early, then Basic, then Euro Step (after its long second step), and Finger Roll comes out late.
  - Your **Layup** rating sets the window, and traffic shrinks it.
  - A green layup is a big boost, but contact can still beat it.
  - A quick tap still gives you an untimed layup, the way it always played.
- **Jumper timings vary:** running jumpers come out quicker, step-backs and fadeaways a little slower, and threes a touch slower than mid-range jumpers. Your ratings still set every window.
- **Hot and cold:**
  - 3 straight greens or finishes at the rim put you **on fire** (a flame under your feet, +6% make chance).
  - Missing 4 wide-open shots makes you **cold** (an ice crystal).
- **Takeovers:** 6 clean plays in a row earn a 60-second position takeover. PG/SG get shooting, SF/PF get finishing, and C gets boards and blocks.

### Defense and rules
- **Out of bounds is called on the painted lines,** like a regulation game, on every court.
  - A ball touching the floor on or past a line is out, a dribble included.
  - A player with a foot on or over a line is out.
  - The half-court line is the back line in half-court games, and a ball over the top of the backboard is out.
- **Stronger defense:**
  - Contests and blocks reach 3.75% further.
  - Positioning, hands up and the driving lane all add pressure.
- **The rebound and block assist** (your jump curves toward the ball) is **3.75% stronger** in the final touches.
- **Only the Posterizer badge knocks defenders down.** Dunkers no longer pass through bodies.
- **Traveling** is called in every mode.

### Movement, stamina and feel
- **Stamina matters:**
  - Sprinting, jumpers, layups, dunks, reaching and jumping drain it. In the final touches, all of these cost **twice as much**, and **screens now cost stamina** too.
  - Repeating the same dribble move or the same mistake drains you faster.
  - Good plays and a high Lock-In grade give stamina back.
- **Game speed:** 1.85% slower than v0.4.4, then a further **0.75%** in the final touches.
- **Better collision:**
  - The ball has a body now: nobody can dribble it through a defender's legs or chest.
  - Players don't end up inside each other in a crowd.
- **Dribbling** varies by Ball Handle, speed and height.
- **Attack the rim** takes off from further out with an open lane.

### AI
- **AI players are 10% smarter in every tier:** better reads, quicker reactions, tighter defense and better shot selection.
- **After an ankle-breaker:**
  - Slashers, playmakers and bigs take it straight to the rim.
  - Sharpshooters, stretch bigs and lockdowns rise up right there.
- **Fewer pointless kick-outs:** a driver who's cut off takes the floater or pull-up when it's there, and only passes to an open teammate with a better look.
- **Alley-oops** only go to teammates who can actually get there. Nobody flies in from half court any more.

### Auto-play
- **Auto-play (H) stays on** from one game to the next (park, Pro-Am, the Pro Run and crew runs) until you turn it off.

## Progression and economy
- **Badges:**
  - Each tier is a bigger step up than the last.
  - A stat with none of its badges plays 10% weaker.
- **90 OVR cap with cap breakers:**
  - Each of your first 5 Hall of Fame badges brings 5 cap breakers: +1 to a maxed-out attribute, past the cap.
  - A build holds up to 7 Hall of Fame badges, and the 7th unlocks your archetype's **Icon badge**, with its own exclusive animation.
- **Cheaper and richer:**
  - Store prices, upgrades and boost packs cost 35% less.
  - Games pay 35% more VC.
  - The park streak bonus is doubled (up to 4x).
- **Results** in the park continue by themselves after 10 seconds.

## Animations
- **32 new animation packages:**
  - jump-shot bases: Fadeaway, Hitch, Slingshot, Scissor Kick, Knee Tuck and Sway;
  - releases: Wave, Statue, Snatch Back, Old School, Point and Cobra;
  - dunk packages: Hammer, Liberty, Scoop, Hand Switch, Half Spin and **Eastbay**;
  - size-ups: Pound, Snake, Crab, Stutter and Showtime;
  - celebrations: Goggles, Dust Off, Airplane, Mic Drop, Take a Bow and Roar;
  - a new **layup** slot: Basic, Euro Step, Finger Roll and Scoop.
- **Every package is its own,** in the store preview and in games:
  - Each jump-shot base carries the ball on its own path. Silk got its own base.
  - Each release has its own follow-through, release time and launch angle, held even after you land.
  - Each dunk package has a signature finish nobody else's has. New signatures for Rim Rocker, Contact Dunks, Showtime and High Flyer.
  - Each size-up has its own dribble height, width, rhythm and combos.
- **Icon badge animations** can't be bought. Your Icon badge brings its own.

## Performance and fixes
- **No more hitching or freezing in the parks.**
  - The cause was building new people mid-frame, worst at the King Tut Cup.
  - Players are now reused, or built a little at a time.
  - In testing at the Cup:

    | | Before | After |
    |---|---|---|
    | Court changeover | about 1 second | a couple of milliseconds |
    | People arriving | about 2 seconds | a few milliseconds |
    | Leaving the court | about 1 second | a few milliseconds |

  - Shaders are now compiled ahead of time.
- **Badges** upgraded in a game now show and play at their new tier in the very next "run it back" game. They used to keep the old tier until you left the court.
- **The Ante-up message** at the start of a Cup game now shows.
- **The game keeps running** when you click out of the window. Turn Pause on focus loss on in Settings if you want it to pause.
- **25 soundtrack tracks.**
