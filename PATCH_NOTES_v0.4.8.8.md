# RIMRIFT — v0.4.8.8 Patch Notes: The Pro-Am Overhaul

v0.4.8.8 turns Pro-Am into a season-long team mode. You make or join a Pro-Am team, play series against other teams at 12 new Pro-Am parks, climb the ranks together, and earn rank rewards that show in every mode. Your team has its own HUB: a gym that grows into a clubhouse as you rank up.

**Season 1 starts the moment the server runs v0.4.8.8 and lasts 45 days.** Everyone starts it in Bronze.

Everyone has to be on v0.4.8.8 to play online together. Pull the update before you connect.

---

## Pro-Am teams

- **Your team:** opening the Pro-Am tab asks you to **Create a Pro-Am team** or **Join one**. You can search every team on the server, player teams and AI teams alike, by name or abbreviation. Each result shows the team's rank, record and players.
- **Creating a team:** pick a name, abbreviation, colors, logo, jerseys and a **home court** from the 12 Pro-Am parks.
- **Roster:** a team holds up to 10 players, with 5 on the floor. You can be on one team at a time. If you leave, you keep your own rank and RP.
- **Joining:** teams are open to anyone by default. The founder can make the team invite-only, and then he accepts players who ask to join. The founder owns the settings and can hand the team on.
- **Team Captain:** whoever is hosting the team's session is the Captain. That's the teammate who has been in the HUB longest. The Captain queues games and runs the draft.
- **AI teams:** 28 AI Pro-Am teams play on every server, spread across the ranks. They're made of real AI players from the server's world, wear league jerseys, and play their own series against each other through the day, so the standings move.

## Series, ranks and RP

- **Series:** your team plays a series in its rank. Each game is matchmade on its own, and the games alternate home and away, starting at home.

  | Rank | Series |
  | --- | --- |
  | Bronze | 3 games, first to 2 wins |
  | Silver | 5 games, first to 3 |
  | Gold and up | 7 games, first to 5 |

- **Matchmaking:** you're matched first with another real team in the queue within 2 divisions of yours. After about 45 seconds with no match, you play the AI team nearest your rank.
  - **Two real teams:** they play one shared online game, with everyone in it.
  - **Where:** a home game is played at your home court. An away game is at your opponent's court.
- **Divisions:** each rank has divisions.

  | Rank | Divisions |
  | --- | --- |
  | Bronze | 3 |
  | Silver | 5 |
  | Gold | 7 |
  | Platinum | 7 |

  Diamond, Master and GrandMaster come later.
- **Winning a series** promotes the whole team one division at once. About 3 series wins take you from Bronze to Silver, 5 from Silver to Gold, and 7 from Gold to Platinum.
  - It also pays a series bonus: 1,500 VC in Bronze, 3,000 in Silver, 5,000 in Gold and up.
- **RP:** every player has 0–100 RP in his division. Wins, losses and how you played still move it after every game. Reaching 100 RP promotes you a division between games.
- **Losing a series** puts the whole team and every player in it at 0 RP in their division.
  - **Demotion:** a player who was **already at 0 RP** when the series was lost drops a division. Otherwise players only lose RP from losses and poor performance, and never go below 0.
  - **Forfeits:** forfeiting or leaving a game counts as a loss (−20 RP), and the series carries on.

## The GM draft

- **The clipboard:** in the HUB office, the Captain can **draft** players to fill his team's empty spots for a series.
- **The list:** the clipboard shows **5 randomized real AI players** from the server's world, from near your rank and across the positions.
- **Refreshes:** you get **3 free refreshes** of the list per series, and they come back when the series ends.
- **Picks:** a player you pick fills that spot for the whole series. At the ready check, any spot that's still empty is filled from the current list.

## The Pro-Am HUB

**Getting there:** go in from the Pro-Am tab ("Go to the HUB"), or walk into the new **Pro-Am HUB building** in every park. Your teammates in the HUB see each other, and everyone can shoot around while waiting.

- **Bronze, Eastside Middle School:** a small, well-worn one-court gym with folding bleachers and a stage. A small office door sits in the wall behind one hoop.
- **Silver, Northgate High:** a high-school gym with bleachers on both sides, pennants, a scoreboard and a bigger office with a window onto the floor.
- **Gold, your clubhouse:**
  - your team's own building, with an indoor full court, a shootaround court, a lounge, a trophy case and a large glass-fronted office;
  - the founder can change the floor, walls, logo and colors;
  - glass doors lead out to your **home park**, where the park's own clubhouse stands beside the court.
- **On your home court:** you and your teammates can run 3s against the AI, together or against each other. These games pay a quarter of a park game's VC and no Rep.
- **Platinum:** the Gold clubhouse with a Platinum trim. More is coming for the higher ranks.
- **The office:**
  - the **whiteboard** shows the current series game by game (opponent, home or away, score), your record, everyone's rank and RP, and the season countdown;
  - the **clipboard** is the draft;
  - the Captain can **Queue for the next game**. That sends a ready check to everyone in the HUB, fills the empty spots and starts the matchmaker.

## The 12 Pro-Am parks

Every Pro-Am game is played at one of 12 new park courts:

1. **Under the Overpass:** under the highway, with traffic overhead.
2. **Long Beach:** on the sand, with the ocean, a pier and a Ferris wheel.
3. **AAU Gym:** a three-court tournament hall.
4. **Rooftop:** above the city.
5. **Projects:** among brick towers, with people on the stoops.
6. **Riverside:** by the water and the bridges.
7. **Old Warehouse:** an indoor converted warehouse.
8. **Schoolyard:** a school court with murals.
9. **The Cage:** a fenced downtown court.
10. **Maplewood Community Center:** an indoor community gym.
11. **Desert Dusk:** an outdoor court with a mountain sunset.
12. **Night Market:** next to food stalls and lanterns.

The home team's colors and logo are on the court and the banners. Time of day, weather and crowd change from game to game, and every screen in a game sees the same park.

## Rank rewards

Your rank's VC and Rep boost applies in **every mode**. Boosts don't stack: each new rank's boost replaces the one before it.

| Rank | VC and Rep boost | By your name, in every mode | Player introductions | Items (won, never sold) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bronze | none | none | none | none: every team starts here |
| Silver | +2% | Silver rank icon | none | none |
| Gold | +4% | Gold rank icon | Gold borderplate with a sheen | Gold **PRO-AM Chain** with iced-out letters; **Gold Low-Top Canvas** sneakers |
| Platinum | +5% | Platinum rank icon | Animated Platinum borderplate | Platinum **PRO Watch**; Platinum **Headband**; your record over your head in the park (a setting) |
| Diamond, Master, GrandMaster | to come | to come | to come | to come |

- **Items:** a rank's items are yours for good once you reach it, even after a demotion.
- **Following your rank:** the boost, the icon and the borderplate always follow your current rank.
- **Season rewards:** when a season ends, you get a season banner for the highest rank you reached, for example "S1 · Gold". It's shown on your introduction card. Gold players and up also get a Season 1 version of the PRO-AM Chain. Each season's rewards are only ever given out in that season.

## Pro-Am is the most realistic mode

These changes apply in Pro-Am games only:

- **Icon badges:** their effects (and the ICON cursor and aura) are off. Their animation packages still play.
- **Badges:** all badge effects are **10% smaller**.
- **Overall gap:** the gap between builds' overalls is **10% smaller**.
- **Difficulty:** the game, AI included, is **10% harder**.

## Also in this update

- **Got Next:** AI players can't hold a Got Next spot until they're standing on it. If you get to an open spot first, it's yours, even if a group was walking toward it.
- **Season 1 fresh start:** everyone starts Season 1 in Bronze. Your Pro-Am rank from before stays on your profile as history.
